Hello, welcome to my brain!

## I Have Always Enjoyed Teaching
I have always enjoyed math and even majored in it in college and because of this there has always been people that I have helped with the subject. Personally I have tutored friends and professionally I have worked as a Learning Assistant and Mentor for Calculus 1 and 2 in college and as an Instructor at Mathnasium tutoring students on all levels of math from basic addition and subtraction all the way up to Differential Equations. I have always appreciated helping someone finally understand something and seeing my impact on someone else's success is always satisfying. I wanted to start something where I could potentially have the same effect on people when it comes to Development which is the skillset that I have been building for the last several years. 

## Teaching is the Best Way to Learn
The other biggest thing that I have found throughout many types of tutoring is that the best way to learn something and to really prove to yourself that you understand it is to teach it to someone else. Given this I want to prove to myself that I am learning concepts thoroughly enough to write about them in a way that makes sense. This should help clarify things that I need to research more and also solidify the skills that I already have as well as serving as a reference for my future self who may someday need a reminder of a topic I am learning now that I may not use as much down the line.


## What topics can I expect to see?
That is mostly TBD as software development is a massive field and I constantly find myself learning different topics and switching between what my main interest is. However, I am trying to nail down a more specific skillset and there are some languages, tools and frameworks that I can say will definitely by making an appearance in some form. React is the first one that is a main staple throughout my projects and is my go to frontend framework that I will definitely be writing about. I have also been very interested in learning more about Java and Object-Oriented Programming and this has recently extended to working with JEE and Spring Boot as a backend solution since learning that it can easily be used alongside React and on initial use I have liked it more than NodeJS. 

I do not want to make this intro too long especially since who knows who will actually read this but now a bit of an overview of my intentions exists so I can get into the tech of it all.
